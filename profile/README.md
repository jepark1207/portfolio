### 🔬 Research

**Stanford Smart Sensing Systems Lab** — Low-power wildlife camera trap with IR
image acquisition and classification. I own the IR flash driver hardware, the
embedded capture software, the enclosure, and the prototype-to-PCB transition.

**Stanford Space Initiative** — Power team on a rover built to traverse
Antarctica. Designed the power management unit PCB and implemented MPPT solar
tracking for extreme-cold conditions.

### 🛠 Selected projects

**[ShadowHive](https://github.com/jepark1207/portfolio/blob/main/projects/shadowhive.md)** — Covert mapping, positioning, and silent communication.
iPhone LiDAR builds a live floor plan at ~12 fps; UWB anchors track teammates
through walls; facial EMG lets you issue commands by mouthing words with no
sound at all — **88.6% streaming accuracy**, a 22 kB INT8 model on an NPU.

**[ProsthetiSense](https://github.com/jepark1207/portfolio/blob/main/projects/prosthetisense.md)** — Deep learning on non-invasive forearm EMG to drive a
prosthetic hand at **94% accuracy**, attacking the $20k–$80k cost of myoelectric
limbs. *ISEF Finalist · Air Force Research Award · Regeneron Biomedical
Engineering Award.*

**[Custom MP3 Player](https://github.com/jepark1207/portfolio/blob/main/projects/custom-mp3.md)** — A battery-powered music player designed from a blank
schematic: ESP32-S3, I²S DAC, custom PCB, and the firmware and UI to drive it.

**[Preliminary Pancreatic Screening](https://github.com/jepark1207/portfolio/blob/main/projects/pancreatic-screening.md)** — CNN screening for pancreatic
cancer on CT scans, built to run without institutional compute. **96% test
accuracy.**
